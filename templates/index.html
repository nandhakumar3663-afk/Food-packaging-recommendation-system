Core Engineering Pillars

Built with domain science rigor and zero reliance on black-box hype

🛡️

Transparent Rule Engine

Domain rules (rules/packaging_rules.yaml) strictly enforce food safety heuristics. Foods with high fat mandate low Oxygen Transmission Rates (OTR ≤ 50), respiring produce enforces gas ventilation, and low pH excludes reactive containers.

⚖️

Compatibility Scoring Model

A project-defined weighted multi-attribute compatibility score (0.0 to 100.0%) balances gas barrier, moisture protection, mechanical strength, sealability, shelf life, recyclability, and economic cost per square meter.

📚

Verified Packaging Database

Every material catalog entry contains explicit measurement test conditions (e.g. 23°C 50% RH for OTR, 38°C 90% RH for WVTR) and literature citations (Robertson, Massey, Rhim). Synthetic benchmark records are visibly flagged.

💡

Explainable Decision Support

Every recommendation explains why the film or container was chosen, which food properties triggered the constraints, and returns neutral alternatives (Recommended, Alternative, Lower-cost, Sustainability-oriented).
