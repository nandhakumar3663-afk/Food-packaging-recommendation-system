Why Use This System?

🛡️ Domain Safety Rules

Hard constraints prevent unsafe recommendations (e.g., bare aluminum on high-acid foods or non-permeable films for respiring fruits).

🤖 Machine-Learning Guidance

A fast Random Forest model assists candidate scoring based on training patterns, running locally on CPU.

🌿 Sustainability & Cost

Automatically ranks lower-cost and high-recyclability alternatives alongside the primary recommended match.

🔍 100% Explainable

No black-box answers. View exact reasons, barrier values, triggered rules, and literature citations.

💡 Methodology & Performance Note:

This application uses verified packaging data from Robertson (2012) and Massey (2003) alongside demonstration synthetic test cases. Compatibility scores reflect project-defined multi-criteria ratings and should not be used as clinical shelf-life certification. Designed for CPU execution on AMD Ryzen 5 5500U.
